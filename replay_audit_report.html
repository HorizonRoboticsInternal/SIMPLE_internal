Does replay reproduce the recording?

Ten psi_new_0828 datasets, 801 episodes replayed end to end through the decoupled-WBC pipeline and measured against the recordings they came from.

Q1 · Bit-exact reproduction?

No — and not even repeatable.

The scene restores to ≤1 mm — then physics takes over. The same episode replayed twice differs by 0.063 rad, because the pipeline runs on the wall clock. It is a re-simulation, not a playback.

Q2 · Does it finish the tasks?

Often — but it is not finishing the demo.

Per-task success spans 16–84%. Where the recording can be scored too, replay agrees with its verdict on only 26–57% of episodes: it fails demos that worked and passes demos that never did.

First, a distinction

Two different things in this repo are called “replay”

They answer the two halves of the question in opposite ways.

Which joints, exactly

All 43 joints logged individually over 48 replayed episodes (12 per task). The divergence concentrates in exactly two places. The sagittal stepping chain — knees (0.26–0.28 rad), ankle pitch, hip pitch — symmetric left/right, sustained (high means), and growing with locomotion. And the right-hand fingers (0.11–0.18 rad peaks with near-zero means): brief spikes as the grasping hand meets contact at slightly different moments. The left hand, which never grasps in these tasks, sits at exactly 0.000 — and shoulders, elbows and wrists track within a few hundredths. Your own recording ranks the same way: knees first (0.36 / 0.31 rad), then hip and ankle pitch.

Root causes

What is wrong with the recordings

1 · The operator’s turning command is not in the dataset

Blocking

teleop.navigate_command[2] is not the joystick — it is the controller’s own derivative, computed from a two-frame-stale yaw. Exact on all 403 episodes / 158,300 frames:

vyaw[t] == clip( wrap( target_yaw[t] − robot_yaw[t−2] ) / 0.5, ±1 )

BendPick 100.0% exact  XMoveBendPick 100.0%  LocoPickBetweenTables 100.0%  BendHandover 100.0%
mean |difference| = 0.00000 rad/s at lag 2  ·  0.2% match at lag 0

Replay reads it back as the turning flag: the 0.1 gate silently drops the 38–60% of frames carrying sub-gate turn intent, and the Balance⇆Walk switch fires at different moments than it did during collection. The loss is unrecoverable — forcing the gate open makes things worse (XMoveBendPick 30/40 → 21/40). The real-robot recordings use the opposite convention: same column, two meanings, split by commit 5b304e4.

Code: g1_gear_wbc_policy.py:258 (reads slot 2 as vyaw_flag) · :286 (the 0.1 gate) · :316 (policy switch) · replay_decoupled_agent.py:151 (passes the column through unchanged)

2 · The success bar sits above what the demonstrations achieve

Blocking

BendPick demands an 8 cm lift. The demonstrations top out at 7.5 cm:

recorded lift, 100 episodes — deciles: 0.030  0.047  0.057  0.060  0.063  0.066  0.075 m
episodes clearing 0.080 m: 0 / 100

A bit-exact replay would score 0%; the observed 74% is replay overshooting the demo. Success-rate numbers here measure threshold calibration as much as replay fidelity.

Code: g1_wholebody_bend_pick_teleop.py:278–286, _LIFT_HEIGHT = 0.1, metadata["success_criteria"] = 0.8

3 · The re-recorded dataset labels a diverged robot with the original numbers

Blocking

_build_frame copies every column verbatim from the source row and swaps in only the freshly rendered image — the labels describe the recording while the pixels show the replay, by then 28–120 mm away at the target object. A systematic image/label offset, baked into the training set. The kinematic render_decoupled_wbc path does not have this problem.

Code: replay_decoupled_wbc.py:191–228 _build_frame · :662 the call site

4 · The replay clock is wall-clock, so replay is not repeatable

Material

Goals are stamped with time.monotonic() and the interpolator evaluates at wall time — the trajectory depends on how fast the machine happened to run. Same episode, same process, twice:

ep 0 — identical at step 0, then max |Δq| 0.063 rad, final object z differs 1.74 mm
ep 3 — identical at step 0, then max |Δq| 0.034 rad, final object z differs 0.54 mm

Clocking the interpolation by frame index would make replay deterministic — the precondition for any fidelity number.

Code: replay_decoupled_agent.py:141–199 · interpolation_policy.py:130–134

5 · The recorded action is a controller output, re-fed as a controller input

Material

action stores the interpolator’s output (waist slots overwritten by the IK target); replay hands it back to the same interpolator as a goal. The signal is interpolated twice, on top of the 0.085 rad median tracking offset already present in the recording.

Code: teleop_decoupled_wbc.py:141–170 (records the output) · replay_decoupled_agent.py:163–199 (re-feeds it)

6 · action and observation.state use different joint orders

Minor · trap

Identical for indices 0–21, different from 22 on: action follows the robot-model order, observation.state follows WHOLE_BODY_JOINTS. Both are named correctly in meta/info.json — but any code assuming a shared layout silently compares left-hand joints against right-arm joints.

Check with: info.json → features.action.names vs features["observation.state"].names

7 · Two bugs in the scoring code itself

Minor

Rewards accumulate twice per step — step() calls compute_reward, then check_success calls it again, so streak tasks reach their criterion at half the intended dwell (BendHandover: recorded max reward exactly 0.585 vs criterion 0.600). The container check is stale — check_object_in_container compares spawn-time layout poses that are never updated from the sim, and a precedence slip leaves one contact ordering unguarded by the xy test.

Code: sonic_loco_manip.py:172–179 · g1_wholebody_locomotion_pick_between_tables_teleop.py:316–346

If the goal is fidelity

What would have to change

  • Want the recorded trajectory? Use render_decoupled_wbc — exact, label-consistent.
  • Keep recording from the physics path? Re-label from the replayed sim in _build_frame, not from the source.
  • Record both turning channels — the operator flag and the executed rate. One slot cannot carry both.
  • Clock the interpolation by frame index, not time.monotonic().
  • Calibrate success_criteria against the demos — BendPick’s bar exceeds every one of them.

Method & reproduction

Harnesses under data/replay_audit/ mirror the episode loop of src/simple/cli/replay_decoupled_wbc.py — same config, agent, stabilisation gate and use_policy_action — run headless with --sim-mode mujoco. Datasets were copied off the NAS first; per-episode JSON lives in data/replay_audit/out/ and all6/. Success is SonicLocoManipEnv._success, exactly as the shipping CLI reports it.

Clips: replay_video.py re-simulates with the repo’s VideoRecorder attached (frame-aligned to the recording), encode_media.py trims and compresses, and build_video_section.py regenerates the side-by-side section. Media lives in data/replay_audit/report_media/ — keep it beside this file.
