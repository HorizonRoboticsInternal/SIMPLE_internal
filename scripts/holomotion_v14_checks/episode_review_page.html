Physical columns are the simulation's values, not clipped. The SDK columns are the Revo 2 Touch fields exactly as recorded (tactile.{side}.force_normal, force_tangential, force_direction, proximity, status): forces in 0.01 N counts that saturate at 2500 (25 N), the tangential direction in degrees clockwise from the fingertip seen from outside the pad (65535 = no tangential force), proximity as capacitance counts (more = closer, 0 = nothing within 1 cm). About this episode

Images and tactile values come from the filled dataset: the lean recording's bit-exact replay, with the head camera, the wrist cameras and the Revo 2 tactile pads computed on each frame's recorded state (replay_holomotion_v14 --fill). Every recorded tactile column is shown: the 3×3 taxel grid of each pad with its normal channel and its two shear channels (taxels_N; a simulation extra, the real sensor reports one value per pad, and unreliable as recorded: see the pad panel's "Recorded taxel grid" view), the pad totals in newtons (force_normal_N, force_tangential_N, proximity_m, contacts) and the SDK fields. Pads are drawn seen from outside, fingertip up, the view the SDK's direction angle uses. Video and tactile are sampled every 40 ms (every second recorded frame).
